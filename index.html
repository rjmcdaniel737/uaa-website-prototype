Anthropology

B.A. B.S.

Study the full breadth of human experience — from ancient civilizations to modern cultural systems. UAA's anthropology program emphasizes fieldwork in Alaska and the Arctic, offering unparalleled research opportunities.

Students engage in archaeological digs, linguistic fieldwork, and cultural studies across Alaska Native communities.

About UAA

The University of Alaska Anchorage is Alaska's largest university, serving more than 16,000 students through a broad range of associate, baccalaureate, and graduate programs.

Located in Anchorage — Alaska's largest city — UAA offers a unique urban campus experience surrounded by mountains, wilderness, and a vibrant, multicultural community.

  • ✅ Regionally accredited by NWCCU
  • ✅ Minority-Serving Institution
  • ✅ R2 Research University
  • ✅ Military & Veteran Friendly
